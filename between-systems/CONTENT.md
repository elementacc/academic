## (01) Research / Selected work

**Section introduction:** Studies of spatial thinking, interactive systems, and the ways we come to understand them.

**Project filters:** All · Spatial interaction · Visualization · Methods & interfaces

### 01. Drawing without a page

**Project area:** VR Sketching  
**Project type:** Longitudinal study  
**Filter categories:** Spatial interaction; Methods & interfaces

**Lead**

How are spatial relationships made when there is no surface to draw on?

**Short description**

Immersive 3D sketching examined through interaction traces, construction sequences, movement, and participants’ reflections.

**Project detail (expandable window)**

A longitudinal study of spatial drawing in immersive VR. The research follows how people establish relationships between shapes, viewpoints and body movement when sketching without a physical page or fixed construction procedure.

**Visual status:** Illustrative study / 01. Current graphic is not a research artifact.

### 02. Subcortical Matters

**Project area:** Visualization  
**Project type:** Collaborative work  
**Filter categories:** Visualization; Methods & interfaces

**Lead**

Making complex anatomical information more navigable and interpretable.

**Short description**

Interdisciplinary work on visual interaction with complex scientific and neuroanatomical material across immersive and screen-based contexts.

**Project detail (expandable window)**

An interdisciplinary exploration of complex neuroanatomical representations and the different demands placed on immersive and screen-based visualization, navigation, and interaction.

**Visual status:** Illustrative study / 02. Current graphic is a conceptual illustration.

### 03. TopoVox

**Project area:** Interfaces  
**Project type:** Experimental  
**Filter categories:** Spatial interaction; Visualization

**Lead**

Interfaces for seeing and exploring complex spatial data.

**Short description**

An exploration of visual organization, spatial navigation, and interaction with three-dimensional representations.

**Project detail (expandable window)**

Design inquiry into how interactive spatial representations may support exploration, comparison, and interpretation of complex digital material.

**Visual status:** Conceptual / 03. Current graphic uses coordinate axes and concentric forms.

### 04. Speculative Realities Lab

**Project area:** Experiments  
**Project type:** Research practice  
**Filter categories:** Methods & interfaces

**Lead**

Prototypes, methods, and experiments at the edges of emerging interfaces.

**Short description**

Exploring how speculative approaches can make unfamiliar technological possibilities tangible and discussable.

**Project detail (expandable window)**

Explorations through prototypes and speculative practices to develop questions about emerging computational environments and their relationships to human experience.

**Visual status:** Illustrative study / 04. Current graphic is illustrative imagery.

## (02) About / Approach

### Between disciplines, between perspectives.

The work begins with questions about perception and action: what do people notice, what do they use as a reference, and how does an interface change what becomes possible?

**Working methods**

Experimental studies, interviews, behavioral and interaction logs, visualization prototyping, iterative design, and multimodal analysis.
