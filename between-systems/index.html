№01.

VR Sketching

How are spatial relationships made when there is no surface to draw on?

Immersive 3D sketching examined through interaction traces, construction sequences, movement, and participants’ reflections.

A longitudinal study of spatial drawing in immersive VR. The research follows how people establish relationships between shapes, viewpoints and body movement when sketching without a physical page or fixed construction procedure.

№03.

Movement-based research

Translating continuous movement qualities into discrete, programmable functions for an immersive drawing interface.

Body-Sketch: hand gestures, full-body movement and eye drawing as inputs for 3D sketching beyond hand controllers.

An ongoing system development (2023–present) with students of HTW Berlin, IKG. Users draw through a wide range of motion inputs, from subtle hand gestures to full-body interaction, and through gaze — broadening input modalities and the accessibility of immersive drawing.
