1/1 Studio is a creative consultancy with over a decade of experience. Originally a historian, the studio carefully weaves European art history and design into every frame. 1/1's imagery distills the essence of subjects in an eloquently editorial yet easy going way. The approach is empathic and thoughtful, laying emphasis on the individuality and personality of subjects.

The work is the testament to an artful blend of style and substance, and a keen eye for curation. 1/1's vision and committed personal approach is sought after by private clients across the globe. The studio has traveled extensively for projects - from Sweden to India, Paris to New York City, Greece to Mexico, Iceland to Spain.

The work has appeared in numerous international publications like Harper's Bazaar and Anti-Bride.
