Project Overview

NE GEURRA is a comprehensive brand identity project that encompasses multiple touchpoints including visual identity, art direction, printed materials, and digital presence. The project focuses on creating a cohesive brand experience across all mediums.

[01.1] Branding

Complete visual identity system including logo design, color palette, typography selection, and brand guidelines. The branding establishes a strong foundation for all subsequent design applications.

[01.2] Art Direction

Creative direction for photography, illustration style, and overall visual language. This ensures consistency across all brand communications and marketing materials.

[01.3] Printed Matter

Design and production of business cards, letterheads, brochures, packaging, and other print collateral. Each piece reinforces the brand identity through careful attention to materials and finishing.

[01.4] Website

Digital platform design and development that translates the brand identity into an engaging online experience. The website serves as the primary digital touchpoint for the brand.

Client: NE GEURRA
Year: 2024
Services: Branding, Art Direction, Print Design, Web Design

ONWARD represents a complete e-commerce solution combining strategic design thinking with technical implementation. The project delivers a comprehensive digital commerce platform with supporting brand materials.

[03.1] E-Commerce Design

User experience and interface design for the online store, focusing on conversion optimization and intuitive navigation. The design prioritizes both aesthetics and functionality.

[03.2] E-Commerce Development

Technical implementation of the e-commerce platform including payment integration, inventory management, and responsive functionality across all devices.

[03.3] Digital Assets

Creation of digital marketing materials, social media templates, email campaigns, and online advertising assets that support the e-commerce platform.

[03.4] Print Assets

Physical materials including packaging design, shipping labels, promotional materials, and brand collateral that complement the digital experience.
