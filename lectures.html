Project Overview

APEX, STRUCTURE I explores the relationship between form and material through ceramic sculpture. This installation piece investigates how unglazed ceramic surfaces interact with light and space, creating dynamic shadows and textural contrasts.

Material Research

The work focuses on the raw, unglazed ceramic surface, emphasizing the natural texture and color variations that emerge during the firing process. The material choice reflects a commitment to authenticity and tactile experience.

Spatial Considerations

Positioned as an installation, the piece transforms its environment through careful consideration of scale, proportion, and viewer interaction. The work invites contemplation of architectural and sculptural relationships.

Medium: Unglazed Ceramic
Dimensions: 26 x 14 x 8cm
Type: Installation

Series Overview

RESISTANCE, FORMATION I-VII is a comprehensive series exploring themes of resilience and adaptation through ceramic forms. Each piece in the series represents a different approach to material resistance and formal evolution.

Conceptual Framework

The series investigates how materials respond to stress, pressure, and transformation. Each formation represents a moment of equilibrium between opposing forces, captured in ceramic permanence.

Installation Strategy

The seven pieces are arranged to create dialogue between individual forms while maintaining the integrity of each sculptural element. The installation emphasizes both unity and diversity within the series.

Medium: Unglazed Ceramic
Dimensions: Variable
Series: 7 Pieces

Material Investigation

IMITATION, POSITION I-VII challenges traditional material hierarchies by combining industrial materials with concrete. The series explores how synthetic and natural materials can create new formal languages when brought into dialogue.

Material Dialogue

The combination of heat-shrink tubing, PVC, and concrete creates unexpected textural and formal relationships. Each material maintains its inherent properties while contributing to a unified sculptural statement.

Positioning Strategy

The title "Position" refers both to the physical placement of objects in space and the conceptual stance taken regarding material authenticity and imitation in contemporary sculpture.

Materials: Heat-shrink tubing, PVC, Concrete
Dimensions: Variable
Duration: 2021-2022

Conceptual Framework

SOFT-DEFENSE explores the paradox of protection through vulnerability. Using ceramic's inherent fragility as a metaphor for defensive mechanisms, the work questions traditional notions of strength and security.

Defensive Strategies

The installation employs ceramic forms that appear both protective and vulnerable, creating a visual tension that reflects contemporary anxieties about safety, boundaries, and personal space.

Temporal Development

Developed over the course of a year during 2020-2021, the work reflects the evolving understanding of protection and isolation during a period of global uncertainty.

Medium: Unglazed Ceramic
Concept: Protection through Vulnerability
Context: Pandemic Response

Photographic Series

SACRIFICE represents a departure into analog photography, exploring themes of loss, transformation, and renewal through black and white imagery. The series investigates what must be given up in the process of creation and growth.

Analog Process

The commitment to analog photography reflects a deliberate choice to embrace the unpredictable and irreversible nature of the medium, mirroring the themes of sacrifice and commitment explored in the work.

Print Matter

The series exists both as individual photographs and as a cohesive publication, allowing for different modes of engagement with the work and expanding its reach beyond gallery spaces.

Medium: Analog Photography
Format: Black and White Series
Publication: Print Matter
