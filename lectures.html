Project Overview

APEX, STRUCTURE I explores the relationship between form and material through ceramic sculpture. This installation piece investigates how unglazed ceramic surfaces interact with light and space, creating dynamic shadows and textural contrasts.

Material Research

The work focuses on the raw, unglazed ceramic surface, emphasizing the natural texture and color variations that emerge during the firing process.

Spatial Considerations

Positioned as an installation, the piece transforms its environment through careful consideration of scale, proportion, and viewer interaction.

Medium:Unglazed Ceramic
Dimensions:26 x 14 x 8cm
Type:Installation

Series Overview

RESISTANCE, FORMATION I-VII is a comprehensive series exploring themes of resilience and adaptation through ceramic forms.

Conceptual Framework

The series investigates how materials respond to stress, pressure, and transformation. Each formation represents a moment of equilibrium between opposing forces.

Installation Strategy

The seven pieces are arranged to create dialogue between individual forms while maintaining the integrity of each sculptural element.

Material Investigation

IMITATION, POSITION I-VII challenges traditional material hierarchies by combining industrial materials with concrete.

Material Dialogue

The combination of heat-shrink tubing, PVC, and concrete creates unexpected textural and formal relationships.

Positioning Strategy

The title "Position" refers both to the physical placement of objects in space and the conceptual stance taken regarding material authenticity.

Conceptual Framework

SOFT-DEFENSE explores the paradox of protection through vulnerability. Using ceramic's inherent fragility as a metaphor for defensive mechanisms.

Defensive Strategies

The installation employs ceramic forms that appear both protective and vulnerable, creating a visual tension reflecting contemporary anxieties.

Temporal Development

Developed over the course of a year during 2020-2021, the work reflects the evolving understanding of protection and isolation.

Medium:Unglazed Ceramic
Concept:Protection through Vulnerability
Context:Pandemic Response
