WHAT DRIVES YOUR CREATIVE PROCESS?

Material research is the foundation of my practice. I spend countless hours exploring textures, weights, and surfaces. Each material tells a story and carries its own emotional weight. The way light hits a surface, how it feels under your fingertips, the sound it makes when manipulated - these are all crucial elements in my design process.

I believe in letting the materials guide the design rather than forcing them into predetermined forms. This organic approach often leads to unexpected discoveries and innovative solutions. The dialogue between designer and material is what creates truly authentic work.

Sustainability is also a key consideration in my material choices. I seek out recycled, upcycled, and environmentally conscious options whenever possible. The future of design must be responsible and mindful of our planet's resources.

Elena Rodriguez is a Madrid-based material designer and researcher specializing in sustainable design practices. Her work explores the intersection of traditional craftsmanship and contemporary design methodologies. She has exhibited at Design Week Madrid, Milan Design Week, and the London Design Festival. Her current research focuses on bio-materials and circular design principles.
