Selected Work

A curated selection of projects spanning brand identity, editorial design, and digital platforms. Each project represents a unique approach to visual communication and creative problem-solving.

Lukas Morio
Web- and Digital Design
A digital manifestation of a photographers graphical expression
+

Project Overview

Lukas Morio represents a comprehensive digital platform that translates a photographer's unique visual language into an immersive web experience. The project focuses on creating a seamless bridge between analog photography and digital presentation.

The design emphasizes the photographer's distinctive aesthetic through careful consideration of typography, layout, and interactive elements. Each page serves as a canvas that enhances rather than competes with the photographic content.

Technical Approach

The website employs a minimalist design philosophy that prioritizes content visibility and user experience. Custom animations and transitions create a fluid browsing experience that mirrors the photographer's artistic vision.

Responsive design ensures optimal viewing across all devices while maintaining the integrity of the photographic compositions. The platform includes portfolio management, client galleries, and commission inquiry systems.

Client: Lukas Morio
Year: 2024
Services: Web Design, Digital Strategy
Dongle C
Web- and Digital Design
A straightforward and convenient digital experience
+

Design Philosophy

Dongle C embodies the principle that great design should be invisible. The project focuses on creating an intuitive user experience that eliminates friction and enhances productivity for Apple device users.

The interface design prioritizes clarity and functionality, with every element serving a specific purpose in the user journey. Clean typography and strategic use of white space create a calming, professional environment.

User Experience

The platform addresses the common pain points of device connectivity through thoughtful information architecture and streamlined workflows. Users can quickly find solutions and make informed purchasing decisions.

Comprehensive product information, compatibility guides, and customer support integration ensure users have all necessary resources at their fingertips. The design scales seamlessly across desktop and mobile platforms.

Client: Dongle C
Year: 2024
Services: UX/UI Design, E-commerce
Trevor Cheney Gallery
Brand Identity & Digital Platform
Contemporary art gallery digital presence
+

Gallery Identity

Trevor Cheney Gallery required a sophisticated digital presence that would reflect the quality and vision of contemporary art exhibitions. The project encompassed complete brand development and digital platform creation.

The visual identity balances artistic expression with professional presentation, creating a framework that supports diverse artistic voices while maintaining gallery coherence.

Digital Experience

The website serves as both exhibition space and business platform, featuring artist portfolios, exhibition archives, and collector services. High-quality image presentation ensures artworks are displayed with maximum impact.

Custom content management allows gallery staff to easily update exhibitions, artist information, and news. The platform integrates with social media and email marketing systems for comprehensive outreach.

Client: Trevor Cheney Gallery
Year: 2024
Services: Brand Identity, Web Design, CMS
Formative Modern
Editorial Design & Publication
Contemporary design discourse publication
+

Editorial Vision

Formative Modern explores the intersection of contemporary design practice and theoretical discourse. The publication serves as a platform for emerging voices in design criticism and practice.

The editorial design reflects the publication's commitment to experimental approaches while maintaining readability and accessibility. Typography and layout choices support diverse content types from essays to visual portfolios.

Design Approach

The publication design employs a flexible grid system that accommodates various content formats while maintaining visual consistency. Custom typography and careful attention to hierarchy enhance the reading experience.

Both print and digital versions were developed, with the digital platform offering interactive features and expanded multimedia content. The design system scales across different publication formats and special issues.

Client: Formative Modern
Year: 2024
Services: Editorial Design, Typography, Publishing
Space of Time
Installation & Digital Documentation
Temporal art installation documentation
+

Installation Concept

Space of Time explores the relationship between physical space and temporal experience through immersive installation art. The project documents and extends the installation through digital media.

The documentation strategy captures both the static elements and temporal changes within the installation, creating a comprehensive record that serves as both archive and artistic statement.

Digital Extension

The digital platform extends the installation's reach beyond its physical boundaries, offering virtual visitors an opportunity to engage with the work through interactive documentation and supplementary content.

Time-based media, including video documentation and audio recordings, capture the installation's evolution over time. The digital archive serves researchers, critics, and future exhibitions.

Client: Space of Time
Year: 2024
Services: Documentation, Digital Archive, Web Platform
