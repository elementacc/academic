— about

Paulina Greta Stefanovic is a Berlin-based artist, assistant researcher in the HCI field at HTW Berlin, and Ph.D. candidate at TU Berlin. She explores the relationship between technology, art, design and the concepts of space, and human perceptions.

Her focus is on immersive and movement-based interfaces. Her works have been featured at international venues and multimedia festivals. She has been a lecturer in different European academies teaching Technological Arts.
