WHEN DO YOU FEEL THE MOST FREE?

CAN YOU NAME THREE WOMEN WHO ARE SOURCES OF INSPIRATION FOR YOU?

MY GREAT-GRANDMOTHER HAS A CONSTANT PRESENCE IN MY LIFE. SHE PLAYED A PASSIONATE ROLE IN MY UPBRINGING, TEACHING ME ABOUT RESILIENCE AND HOW TO NEVER LET ANYONE GET YOU THROUGH... SHE WAS A HARD WORKER HER WHOLE LIFE, AND IS THIS LOW MAINTENANCE CHILDHOOD AND GRANDMOTHER THAT SHAPED ME. SHE WAS VERY UNCONVENTIONAL AND MYSELF. HER GREAT-GRANDDAUGHTER, HELD HER HAND WHEN SHE DIED. SHE CONTINUES TO INSPIRE ME EVERY DAY THROUGH HER STRENGTH AND DETERMINATION.

ZUZANA ŠRŮMOVÁ IS A JEWELLERY DESIGNER FROM OSLO. SHE MAKES BEAUTIFUL OBJECTS THAT SPEAK TO ME AND INSPIRE ME TO CREATE. HER WORK IS CHARACTERISED BY A UNIQUE INDIVIDUAL CHARACTERISED BY HER BEAUTY AND CURIOSITY. I ASPIRE TO RESPOND TO MATERIALS TO MAKE SOMETHING BEAUTIFUL. SHE IS ALSO A WONDERFUL MOTHERHOOD AND THE NEED TO BALANCE HER PROFESSIONAL LIFE WITH FAMILY LIFE. SHE IS AMAZING TO IDENTIFY WITH HER WORK AND LIFE. SHE IS A CONSTANT SOURCE OF INSPIRATION AND MOTIVATION FOR ME.

GRIMES. I HAVE BEEN A FAN OF GRIMES' MUSIC AND STYLE SINCE HER EARLY DAYS. I CAN STILL REMEMBER HOW I CUT MY FRINGE SO SIMILAR TO HER FIRST YEAR AT UNI BECAUSE SHE LOOKED SO COOL. I LOVE HOW EXPERIMENTAL AND BOLD SHE MOVED DURING HER PERFORMANCES. NOWADAYS, I STILL LISTEN TO HER MUSIC A LOT WHILE WORKING IN MY STUDIO.

WHAT CHILDHOOD DREAM DO YOU STILL WANT TO ACCOMPLISH?

I WANTED TO BE A FASHION DESIGNER WHEN I WAS LITTLE. I USED TO DRAW COLLECTIONS, SPEND HOURS AND HOURS MAKING CLOTHES FOR MY DOLLS OUT OF MAGAZINES AND ALTERED PIECES OF CLOTHES. MY MOTHER ALWAYS ENCOURAGED ME TO PURSUE CREATIVE PATHS. I DREAM ABOUT SPACES RATHER THAN DRESSING PEOPLE. NOWADAYS, I AM WORKING ON FURNITURE AND OBJECT DESIGN.

MARINA MILENTIJEVIĆ IS A BERLIN-BASED SPATIAL DESIGNER AND ART DIRECTOR ORIGINALLY FROM OSIJEK, CROATIA. MARINA CREATES EMOTIONALLY ENGAGING SCENARIOS INSPIRED BY HER RESEARCH ON CULTURAL PHENOMENA AND SOCIAL BEHAVIOURS. HER RECENT WORKS INCLUDE FEEDS WITH ZUZANA ŠRŮMOVÁ AND OTHER LUND GALLERY OSLO EXHIBITIONS. SHE IS CURRENTLY WORKING ON HER FIRST FURNITURE COLLECTION TITLED SOFT OBJECTS.
